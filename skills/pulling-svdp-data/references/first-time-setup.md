# First-time setup

Shared by every SVdP skill. If a skill needs ServWare access or the volunteer list and does not
find it, bring the volunteer through this, then return to what they were doing.

## What they need

A ServWare username and password, issued by the SVdP conference. Not something you can create
for them — if they do not have one, they need to ask their conference president.

## Setting it up

The username and password are entered **once**, in the extension's settings, not in the chat.

Walk them through it in plain words:

1. Open Claude's settings.
2. Go to Extensions.
3. Find **SVdP ServWare** and open its settings.
4. Type the ServWare username and password into the two boxes.
5. Make sure the extension is switched **on** — a newly installed extension starts switched off,
   and nothing works until it is on.

Then run `servware_health` to confirm.

**Never ask a volunteer to type their password into the chat.** If they start to, stop them,
and explain that it goes in the settings box instead, where it is stored securely on their own
computer and is not part of the conversation.

## If sign-in fails

Say that ServWare did not accept the username or password, and suggest checking for a typo or
confirming it still works by signing in to the ServWare website directly. Do not guess at
variations of their password, and do not retry repeatedly — that can lock the account.

## The volunteer list

Nothing to set up. Volunteer names and ids are read from ServWare when needed. It does require
at least one open request to exist, which is normally the case.
